Same macros every day. 1,675 kcal / 150g C / 145g P / 55g F. No cycling, no exceptions.

What's working
+

Body recomposition confirmed -- -3.8 lb fat, +3.5 lb lean between DEXA scans

Lost 3.8 lb of fat while gaining 3.5 lb of lean mass (Jan 7 to Jun 6). This is genuine recomposition in a deficit -- the ideal outcome of consistent heavy strength training.

P

Protein consistency snapped back -- 141.4g average, 98% of target

After July's protein dip, Aug 1-7 averaged 141.4g against the 145g target. Four of seven days cleared 145g, including Aug 3, Aug 4, Aug 6, and Aug 7.

C

Macros are finally close to the plan

The week averaged 1,649 kcal (98%), 156.8g carbs (105%), 141.4g protein (98%), and 51.1g fat (93%). This is much closer to the recomposition lane than the July snapshot.

H

HIIT returned in a meaningful way

HIIT was logged on Aug 1, Aug 2, Aug 3, and Aug 5, with strength on Aug 3, Aug 4, Aug 6, and Aug 7. The plan is no longer just written down; it is showing up in the week.

S

Snapshot tracking now has three records

The dropdown now keeps Aug 8 as latest, with Aug 1 and Jun 14 retained as prior snapshots. That gives the dashboard a real month-to-month memory.

Watchout areas
!

One over-target day still has outsized impact

Aug 7 landed at 2,106.5 kcal, 248.4g carbs, and 62.5g fat, driven by the Indian mixed dinner plate plus a high-carb lunch. The weekly average is strong, but dining-out structure still matters.

V

VAT increased 81% between DEXA scans -- most important watchout

Visceral fat rose from 1.28 to 2.32 lb while all other fat metrics improved. Most likely cause: deep caloric deficit + training load elevating cortisol chronically. A/G ratio of 1.32 should be below 1.0. Next DEXA scan (target Sep) will show if this is reversing.

M

Fiber and micronutrients improved but remain the biggest nutrition gap

Fiber rose to 9.8g average but is still only 26% of target. Vitamin C is 7%, magnesium 16%, potassium 40%, antioxidants 20%, and folate 23%.

R

Recovery is decent but sleep is still capped

Sleep ranged from 5h48 to 7h07, averaging roughly 6h35. HRV ranged 32.19-74.28 ms and RHR stayed tight at 56-59 bpm, so the system is coping but would benefit from more sleep.

Top 3 actions this week
1

Keep the new macro rhythm

Repeat the 1,500-1,700 kcal days with 140g+ protein. Aug 1-6 is the model: close enough to target without the July under-eating problem.

2

Cap restaurant meals with a carb/fat guardrail

For dining out, choose either a high-carb meal or a high-fat meal, not both. Aug 7 is the example to tighten: carbs went to 248g and fat to 62.5g.

3

Add a daily produce block

Put fruit plus vegetables into the meal template every day. The fastest wins are vitamin C, potassium, magnesium, antioxidants, and fiber.
