Top 3 actions this week
1

Pre-decide the alcohol ceiling

For social dinners, choose zero-calorie mixers and cap drinks before arriving. Aug 28 shows that the meal itself could have fit; liquid calories created the overage.

2

Build toward the new 155g protein floor

Add a Slate milk, egg whites, or another lean protein serving whenever the pre-logged day finishes below the new target.

3

Build one daily produce and fiber block

Pair fruit with a full vegetable serving and a higher-fiber carb. This directly addresses the lowest Cronometer scores without changing the calorie target.
