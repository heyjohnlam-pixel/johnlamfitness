Top 3 actions this week

Pre-log every dining-out dinner before 12pm

Jun 12 is a perfect case study. If the Thai dinner had been pre-logged that morning, the cocktail decision would have been visible before it was made. One minute of morning logging prevents the Friday blowout pattern.

When sleep is short, hunger signals dominate decision-making and make it harder to stay on protocol when eating out. Treat 7h of sleep as a training target -- it directly impacts nutrition adherence the following day.
