Top 3 actions this week
1

Log the full day before dinner

Restore complete logging first. Pre-logging dinner makes the 1,905-calorie ceiling visible before restaurant food, drinks, or snacks can turn a small overage into a large one.

2

Keep restaurant days near the fat target

Oct 3 and Oct 8 both ran above 65g fat, while Oct 9 climbed to 251g carbs. Keep earlier meals lean and simple when dinner is less predictable.

3

Set a seven-hour sleep opportunity

Start with a consistent bedtime and avoid stacking hard cardio onto the shortest-sleep days. Recovery is the clearest limiter in this snapshot.
